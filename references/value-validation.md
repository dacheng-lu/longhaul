# Value validation for consequential investment decisions

Use this optional workflow when deciding whether to invest in a product, service, internal
workflow or open-source capability. Skip it for routine tasks and straightforward requests.
A user may explicitly choose exploration or learning without a commercial objective.

## Frame before building

Record a short experiment in the task's own durable state or a separate linked artifact:

- Purpose (commercial, internal, open source, learning or other), target user and situation.
- Current alternative and the weakest material assumption; distinguish observations from guesses.
- Desired outcome and baseline, if known; for commercial work, buyer, willingness to pay,
  route to reach them and delivery/support costs. Mark unknowns as unknown.
- Cheapest discriminating authorized experiment, budget, observation window, sample and
  predeclared continue, adjust and stop conditions. Include contradictory evidence and limits.

Ask only when a missing choice materially changes the work; pursue independent work and make
reversible assumptions explicit. For commercial research, recent revenue, MRR and profit are
separate measures; a competitor's revenue does not prove our acquisition or demand. Use dated,
credible sources and report sample selection; do not infer a population from a leaderboard.

## Observe and decide

Record the actual denominator, source and date, costs (including failed attempts and human
work), negative responses and missing data. Indicate synthetic, self-reported and independently
observed evidence. A completed experiment can find a hypothesis unsupported or inconclusive.
No response is evidence of neither a sale nor consent. Avoid storing personal data or secrets
in shared artifacts; collect or contact people only with appropriate authorization.

Consider continue, narrow/change the offer or audience, change channel, and stop. Recommend
based on the observations, uncertainty, budget and alternatives. Escalate meaningful choices
to the user; do not automatically expand scope. Changed audience, price or promise can make
old observations inapplicable; retain them with their original scope and rerun the relevant test.

Use the normal checkpoint contract for the experiment artifact and evidence. A passing gate
checks bookkeeping, not market truth, customer identity, payment, or the value of an outcome.
A completed learning experiment may be useful even when the tested hypothesis fails.
